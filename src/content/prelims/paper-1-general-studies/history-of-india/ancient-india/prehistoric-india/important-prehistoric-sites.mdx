# Important Prehistoric Sites

> **Prehistoric Sites** are archaeological locations that provide evidence of early human life before the advent of written records. These sites reveal the evolution of human culture through stone tools, rock paintings, settlements, burials, and agricultural practices, making them an important topic for UPSC and JPSC examinations.

</English>
